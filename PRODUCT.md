# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary user is you (or a very small set of colleagues): one person standing at a door or receiving area with an iPhone, temporarily checking in parcels against a prepared Google Sheet. They hold the phone in one hand, aim at 1D barcodes on labels, and glance at the screen for status—not seated at a desk using a dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Confirm which expected tracking numbers have arrived this batch by scanning labels into a Google Sheet ledger. Success means scanning feels fast, on-screen feedback is obvious at a glance, and afterward the sheet clearly separates received vs not-yet-received so misses can be found on a computer.

## Positioning

Phone camera against a Google Sheet: each scan tells you 新已收, 已收过, or 不在清单, and confirmed receives are written to the sheet.

## Brand Personality

干脆, 醒目, 不花哨. Large status color and short copy over decoration. The interface should feel like a warehouse glance tool, not a product tour.

## Anti-references

Must not look like a SaaS admin or analytics dashboard: card walls, sidebars, purple gradients, metric strips, or multi-panel "ops console" layouts.

## Design Principles

1. **Feedback before chrome** — The first viewport answers "what is this goods, and what was the scan result?" — 货品描述 first; outcome via color + 结果水印.
2. **Three outcomes, unmistakable** — 新已收, 已收过, and 不在清单 stay distinct by slab color plus watermark text; 单号 stays low-contrast secondary when the line hits.
3. **Scan screen is the job** — After mount, setup retreats; pending list stays a secondary fold.
4. **Speed serves the floor** — Do not slow the aim → see-color loop with ornamental UI.
5. **Tool disappears into the task** — Familiar, dense, and quiet; never perform like a branded marketing shell.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pragmatic bar: status via large color blocks **and** explicit text labels; honor reduced-motion preferences. No formal WCAG certification target for this temporary tool.
